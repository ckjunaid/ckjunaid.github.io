NIDAR 2025 · All India Rank 1 · Vice captain

Two drone surveillance swarm

Latency Zero's entry to the National Innovation Challenge for Drone Application and Research: a heavy lift drone and a compute drone that fly together, see people from the air and stream 4K video back to the ground.

Result
AIR 1, Best UAV Design, 100+ teams
Role
Vice captain
Mark 1
18 kg drone, 15 kg payload rated, 10 kg flight tested, Jetson Nano
Mark 2
14 kg drone, Jetson Orin Nano on a built in UPS
Vision
Person detection and alignment, YOLOv8 onboard
Link
Long range 4K video transmission
Payload
Swappable on both drones
Endurance
30 minute flight time

Overview

NIDAR asked for drones that help in real missions such as search and surveillance. We built two drones that work as a pair. Mark 1 carries the heavy payload and the long range 4K camera link, and finds and lines up on people using onboard vision. Mark 2 runs a stronger computer that never loses power, thanks to its own UPS.

What we built

  • Mark 1: an 18 kg heavy lift drone rated for 15 kg of payload, flight tested with 10 kg.
  • A Jetson Nano onboard for person detection and alignment, with long range 4K video transmission to the ground.
  • Mark 2: a 14 kg drone with an upgraded Jetson Orin Nano.
  • A built in UPS for the Orin Nano, plus a direct external supply through connectors, so inference keeps running if main power drops.
  • Swappable payload on both Mark 1 and Mark 2, so a mission can change in the field.
  • Tuned for higher endurance: 30 minutes of total flight time.
  • Both drones flown together as a two drone swarm for surveillance.

ADDC · Team captain · Jul 2023 to Aug 2024

Autonomous medicine delivery drone

Latency Zero's entry to the Autonomous Drone Development Challenge: a custom carbon fibre multirotor that flies itself to GPS coordinates and drops a medicine payload.

Role
Team captain
Result
National rank 22
Airframe
Carbon fibre multirotor, 2 kg payload, 10° downward arm tilt
Autonomy
GPS waypoint missions, MAVROS, real time mapping
Tools
Mission Planner, QGroundControl

Overview

The challenge was to build a drone that could deliver medicine without a pilot. We designed and assembled the airframe ourselves, tuned the flight controller, and built a delivery system that flies to GPS coordinates and releases the payload.

What I built

  • Led the team as captain from design through competition.
  • Built a carbon fibre multirotor with 2 kg capacity. Tilting the arms 10° down lowers the centre of gravity and steadies the flight.
  • Configured and tuned the flight controller in Mission Planner and QGroundControl.
  • Built GPS coordinate based delivery, with real time mapping through MAVROS.

IEEE .<hack> 2024 · 1st prize · Team Vanguards

SensWIN, a study partner for blind students

A portable device that replaces heavy Braille books. It reads B-books, takes input from a Braille bead keyboard, raises text on a dynamic Braille display and gives LLM access for independent study.

Result
1st prize, IEEE .<hack> 2024, MACE
Team
Vanguards: Sapnil M, Muhammad Safwan, Junaid C K, Abhinav Krishna N
Hardware
Servo driven Braille cells, 3D printed module, acrylic enclosure
Features
B-book support, Braille bead keyboard, dynamic display, LLM access

Overview

Braille textbooks are heavy and slow to produce, so blind students often depend on others to study. SensWIN puts the books on one portable device and lets the student read, write and ask questions on their own.

What we built

  • A dynamic Braille display: servo motors in a 3D printed module raise and lower the dots.
  • A Braille bead keyboard for typing notes and questions.
  • B-book support, so whole books load onto the device.
  • LLM access, so a student can ask questions and get answers in Braille.
